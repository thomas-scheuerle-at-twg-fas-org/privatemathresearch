We extend our recent construction of an algebraic generating function by introducing a sixth parameter. Our earlier work prescribed four initial Hankel determinants $(\lambda, m, r, \eta)$ and achieved a normalized Somos-4 recurrence with coefficient 1. Here we allow the leading coefficient to vary, producing the recurrence $H_{k+4}H_k = \alpha H_{k+3}H_{k+1} + \tau H_{k+2}^2$. The construction uses a weighted rescaling: setting $q^6 = \alpha$ and applying suitable parameter scalings to the five-parameter solution gives a six-parameter Hankel sequence with the prescribed first four determinants unchanged.

A key distinction emerges: the determinant sequence itself is rational in the six parameters, as is its associated quartic curve. Yet our particular generating function coefficients are not - the coefficient of $x$ is $q^{-5}r$, requiring a sixth root of $\alpha$. Thus three related objects carry different rationality properties: the determinant orbit and curve descend to the rational field, while this transported series does not.

We compare our construction with Chang and Liu's quadratic-orthogonal-pair approach. A direct shift identifies our orbit with theirs, with subdiagonal interface $d_n = B_{n+2}$. The ambiguity in choosing $u$ versus $-u$ (where $u^2 = \alpha$) corresponds exactly to the parity operation $F(x) \mapsto F(-x)$, which preserves all ordinary Hankel determinants but multiplies shifted determinants of order $N$ by $(-1)^N$.
